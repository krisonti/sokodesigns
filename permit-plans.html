What we draft

The drawings your permit actually needs.

A permit set isn't a floor plan. It's a coordinated package a plan reviewer can approve — dimensioned, coded, and complete on the first try.

Site plans with setbacks, easements, and every existing structure on the parcel
Floor plans with full dimension strings and egress
Elevations and sections as the jurisdiction requires
Structural coordination with a licensed engineer when the scope calls for it
Energy compliance — Manual J/S/D and ResCheck
As-builts when there are no existing drawings to work from
Demo plans for anything coming out
Code notes and schedules for the current adopted code cycle
