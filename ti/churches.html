    Zoning & use permit

    Churches, daycares, and schools often need a use permit in commercial and industrial zones, sometimes with a hearing. Weeks to months, so it goes first.

    Zoning

    Change of occupancy

    Retail, office, or warehouse to assembly or educational is the biggest jump in the code. New occupant load, exits, restrooms, and often sprinklers.

    Occupancy

    Sprinklers & alarms

    Assembly spaces at roughly 300 occupants, and most daycares, require sprinklers and alarms. If the building doesn't have them, that's the budget conversation to have first.

    Fire

    Exits & panic hardware

    Two exits above 49 occupants, three above 500, doors swinging out with panic bars, and travel distance measured across the whole floor.

    Classrooms & daycare
