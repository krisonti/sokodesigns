What you're getting

The paperwork the city asks for.

Most Arizona jurisdictions won't issue a permit on new construction or a mechanical change-out without these. Here's what each one does.

J

The load calculation

Works out how much heating and cooling the building actually needs, room by room — based on orientation, insulation, windows, and local design temperatures.

S

The equipment sizing

Takes the Manual J numbers and selects equipment that matches them. This is what stops a house from getting a wildly oversized unit that short-cycles.

D

The duct design

Sizes the duct runs and registers so the air the equipment moves actually reaches the rooms that need it, at the right volume.
