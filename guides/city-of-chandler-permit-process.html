City of Chandler — Permitting Process

What to expect, from submittal to Certificate of Occupancy

Chandler Development Services · Electronic Plan Review
Chandler handles permits online through Chandler Online Permits and its Electronic Plan Review system — apply, submit construction documents, receive correction comments, pay fees, and print the final permit entirely online. Here's the standard path; SOKO manages the whole process for you.

The Standard Process

  1. Submit Online
    The application and construction documents are uploaded through Chandler's online permit / Electronic Plan Review portal.
  2. Plan Review & Corrections
    Building Safety and other disciplines review the plans against adopted codes. Correction comments come back through the portal; we revise and resubmit until approved.
  3. Fees & Permit Issuance
    Once approved, fees are paid online and the permit and approved plans are issued.
  4. Inspections
    Inspections are requested by text, phone, or online using the permit number. Each phase must pass before it's covered or concealed.
  5. Final Inspection & Certificate of Occupancy
    A final inspection signs off the work, and a Certificate of Occupancy is issued where the use requires one.
