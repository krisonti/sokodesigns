Residential New Build — Permit Process

From lot to permit: what a ground-up custom home takes

Phoenix metro · Applies in every Valley jurisdiction
A ground-up custom home is the most involved permit there is — it touches zoning, site/civil, structural, energy, and every trade. The exact portal and timelines depend on your city (see our city guides), but the path below is the same across the Valley. SOKO takes you from raw lot to issued permit.

The Process, Step by Step

  1. Zoning & Site Check
    Confirm the lot's zoning, setbacks, height and lot-coverage limits, easements, and any HOA or overlay rules — before design gets too far down the road.
  2. Design & Construction Documents
    We produce the full permit set: site plan, civil/grading & drainage, architectural, structural with engineering, MEP, and energy compliance.
  3. Submittal
    The complete set is submitted through your city's portal (SHAPE PHX, DIMES, SPUR, One Stop Shop, etc.).
  4. Multi-Discipline Plan Review & Corrections
    Building, structural, energy, and often planning/engineering review the set. Comments come back through the portal; we revise and resubmit until approved.
  5. Fees & Permit Issuance
    Final valuation-based fees are paid and the permit issues with the approved plans. No vertical construction before this point.
  6. Phased Inspections
    Inspected in sequence — footing/stem wall, underground MEP, framing and rough-ins, insulation, and so on — each passing before it's covered.
  7. Final Inspection & Certificate of Occupancy
    The final sign-off releases the Certificate of Occupancy so the home can be lived in.

What a New-Build Permit Set Includes

  • Site plan — footprint, setbacks, easements, driveway, and utilities
  • Civil / grading & drainage — how the lot drains and ties to the street
  • Architectural — floor plans, elevations, sections, and details
  • Structural + engineering — foundation, framing, and load calcs
  • MEP — mechanical, electrical, and plumbing plans
  • Energy compliance — HVAC Manual J / S / D and the required energy report
