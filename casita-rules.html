The one that catches almost everyone.

The state law that opened casitas up applies to cities and counties — not to HOAs. Your CC&Rs can still prohibit a detached structure, a second dwelling, or a rental, and they remain enforceable after the city has approved your permit. You can get a fully approved building permit and still be in violation the day you break ground.

Read your CC&Rs before you spend a dollar on drawings. If your HOA allows it subject to architectural review, we sequence that approval alongside the city submittal so you're not paying to draw something twice.
